Privacy Policy

Welcome to SmartKalvi. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains how we handle information while providing our platform services.

1. Data Collection

We do not collect personal information from students or teachers. You can use our platform without providing names, emails, or other personal identifiers.

2. Cookies & Ads

We display third-party advertisements to support the platform. Cookies or tracking may be used by our ad partners, but SmartKalvi does not personally collect or store your data.

3. Student & Teacher Information

The platform may store minimal data like student names, class levels, and performance for the internal functioning of features like coins, leaderboard, and booked tutors. This data is used solely to improve user experience and is not shared publicly or sold.

4. Third-party Links

Our platform may contain links to other sites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those third parties.

5. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. All changes will be reflected on this page, and users are encouraged to review it periodically.
